### Full description (máx. 4000)
```
Personal T guides your strength training one exercise at a time and logs what you actually do.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a routine (three are included) or build your own.
• The app shows one exercise per screen: a photo of your machine (you take it with the camera), setup, hands and feet, range of motion, breathing, tempo and a rule for adjusting weight.
• Tick each set when you do it and enter weight, reps, minutes or speed, your perceived effort (1–10) and a comment.

ONLY WHAT YOU CONFIRM COUNTS
The plan’s sets, reps and weights are suggestions: they are never logged on their own. What you did, last time’s reference and suggestions are always shown separately.

CLEAR WEIGHTS
Each exercise says whether the weight is per side, per hand or total, in pounds or kilograms, and shows the total (for example, 45 lb per side = 90 lb added).

REST REMINDER
A rest timer between sets and a notification when the rest is over, even with the screen off.

PROGRESS
Per-exercise charts of top weight, volume, reps and effort, personal records and sessions per week.

YOUR WAY
Editable templates, custom exercises with their own card, and photos of your own machines (camera or gallery).

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers. No account needed.
• Your data is stored on your device.
• Automatic on-device backups, copy to your cloud (Drive, Files…) and restore.
• Optional sync for advanced users: it uses your own Supabase project.

SAFETY
Personal T includes a notice: if you feel chest pain, arm heaviness, unusual shortness of breath, dizziness or cold sweat, stop exercising and seek urgent care. It is a logging and guidance tool, not a medical device: it does not diagnose, interpret symptoms or clear you to exercise. Talk to a health professional before training.

The app interface is in Spanish.
```
